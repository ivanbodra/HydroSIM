# HydroSIM EN / PT-BR Terminology Policy

Status: canonical UI terminology policy

## Language boundary

Code, identifiers, comments, GitHub coordination, technical documentation, and Scientific Registry remain English. The learner-facing application supports English and Brazilian Portuguese from the outset, including navigation, controls, explanations, readouts, plot titles, axes, legends, annotations, and messages.

## Established technical terms

Established hydrographic/motion terms such as **Roll, Pitch, Heave, Yaw, and Heading remain in English in the PT-BR interface**.

At the first pedagogically relevant occurrence, provide a concise Portuguese explanation through non-intrusive contextual help (prefer tooltip on hover, with touch/click equivalent). Subsequent use may show only the technical term.

Do not force literal Portuguese replacements where professional usage is better served by the established English term.

## Portuguese nautical terminology

For nautical terminology in Portuguese, use **Arte Naval** as the primary reference. Complement with DHN/CIAARA usage for hydrographic context and established Kongsberg/CARIS terminology for operational interoperability.

Do not canonize uncertain equivalences merely to complete a translation table. Distinguish physical degrees of freedom from static vessel conditions and navigation quantities.

## UI rule

Localization changes presentation, not scientific state. Language switching must not alter or reset scientific/application state.
